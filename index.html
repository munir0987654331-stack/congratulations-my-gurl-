Chapter One

My Girl

where it all began 🎀

🎀 🌷

Congratulations, bride-to-be! 🎉

Before the henna, before the jewels, before the bridal red, you were simply my girl — the one who knew my silliest secrets, laughed at my worst jokes and stayed when it mattered.

We turned ordinary days into the sweetest memories: pink walls, endless talks, dreams we whispered like they were already true. And when I saw you in that "Bride To Be" sash, glowing and a little shy, I knew my girl was about to bloom into a whole new chapter. I have never been prouder of anyone.

A little dua 🤲

May Allah always keep your heart this soft, your smile this bright, and fill every step of your journey with barakah, ease and endless love. Ameen.

Chapter Two

Someone's Mrs.

the day my girl became a bride 💍

💍 ✨

Mubarak, Mrs.! 💞

Marigold yellow, royal blue, bridal red and a soft sea-glass teal — you wore every colour like it was stitched just for you. I watched you walk into your new life with the same gentle heart I've always known, and I couldn't stop smiling.

I won't lie: a tiny part of me missed my girl that day. But a much bigger part watched you laugh, saw how safe you looked, and knew you were exactly where your heart wanted to be. Marriage didn't take you away from me — it just gave you more love to hold. And somewhere out there, someone got the best part of my best friend.

A dua for your marriage 🤲

بَارَكَ اللَّهُ لَكُمَا وَبَارَكَ عَلَيْكُمَا وَجَمَعَ بَيْنَكُمَا فِي خَيْرٍ

May Allah bless you both, shower His blessings upon you, and keep you united in goodness. May your home be filled with mercy, laughter, patience and peace. Ameen.

🌙
Chapter Three

Now a Mama

the sweetest chapter yet 🍼

🍼 🧸

Congratulations, Mama! 🍼💗

From a shy bride in red to a mama with a tiny hand wrapped around her finger — I've watched you grow into the softest, strongest version of yourself. The way you look at your little one is the same way you've always loved the people in your life: completely, quietly, with your whole heart.

I've seen you tired, nervous, hopeful and so, so brave, and you were beautiful through all of it. Your little one didn't just get a mother — they got the safest place in the whole world. I am honoured to be your friend, and I already love your baby with all my heart.

A dua for your new family 🤲

رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا

"Our Lord, grant us from among our spouses and offspring comfort to our eyes, and make us leaders for the righteous." (Surah Al-Furqan 25:74)

May Allah make your little one the coolness of your eyes and a source of joy for you both, protect them from every evil eye, and raise them upon goodness and light. May He bless your husband, your baby and you with health, peace, and a home full of love and barakah. Ameen, ya Rabb. 🤍

and one last thing…

No matter how life changes,
you'll always be my girl. 🤍

— Forever yours, Ashmal
